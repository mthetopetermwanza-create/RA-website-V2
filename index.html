Organisation overview

Vision — To make reflexology an acclaimed complementary therapy.

Mission — To unify and advocate for reflexology therapists and promote reflexology as a valuable complementary therapy through holistic support and non-invasive touch therapy.

Core values — Professionalism, Compassion, Openness, Diversity, Inclusion, Excellence.

Impact — Training practitioners, community-based care, empowering women and youth, and supporting refugees and host communities in Dzaleka and beyond.

Our story — Rooted in HUSID, a grassroots initiative offering Reflexology voluntarily. Founded in 2022. Officially registered as an NGO with the Ministry of Justice and Constitutional Affairs on September 19, 2025 (Trustees Incorporation Act of 1962, registration link No. D6HPJ5R).

Who we are

A professional community rooted in care

Vision

“To make reflexology an acclaimed complementary therapy.”

Mission

To unify and advocate on behalf of reflexology therapists and to promote reflexology as a valuable complementary therapy through holistic support and natural healing processes for human wellbeing, using non-invasive touch therapy to stimulate specific reflex points and support physical, emotional and energetic balance.

RA strengthens the professional identity and responsible practice of reflexology, increases public awareness, builds partnerships, and develops specialised programmes — including the Integrative Reflexology Center — for vulnerable populations. Through the Simplified-Energetic Plethora Method (S-EPM), practitioners address conditions such as hypertension, kidney-related concerns, stroke recovery support, diabetes-related discomfort, asthma, lumbago, trauma-related stress and other non-communicable disease concerns, always as complementary care alongside existing health services.

Displacement, trauma and limited resources create barriers to healthcare in Dzaleka and surrounding areas. Reflexology offers a safe, affordable, medication-free complementary approach that can be delivered in community settings — with professional standards and clear referral pathways.
